How you earn points

Your trading is already earning you points.

TMGM Rewards points are a thank-you for the trading you already do. Here’s how they work.

  1. Step 1

    You trade

    Your trades earn TMGM Rewards points.

  2. Step 2

    You earn points

    They show in your points balance in the client portal.

  3. Step 3

    You get cash back

    130 points = USD 5, paid into your trading account.

Points per lotTo confirm

  • Forex and gold10 pointsper standard lot
  • Indices, crude oil and crypto1 pointper lot
  • Shares0Shares don’t earn points.

Closed trades held for at least 2 minutes count. Points usually arrive the next day.To confirm

03

Where to check your points

Your points balance is at the top of the client portal. To see the cash back amounts, open TMGM Rewards in the sidebar and go to Cash Back.

Find it: Client portal›TMGM Rewards›Cash Back

04

How to redeem

Six simple steps in the client portal.

  1. 1

    Log in to the client portal and open TMGM Rewards in the sidebar.

  2. 2

    Go to Cash Back.

  3. 3

    Pick the amount you want.

  4. 4

    Press Redeem Now.

  5. 5

    Choose the trading account you want it paid into.

  6. 6

    Confirm.

Your cash back is processed within 1 to 2 working days and paid into the trading account you chose.

Find it: Client portal›TMGM Rewards›Cash Back

05

Questions

How do I earn points?

On your trades: 10 points per standard lot on forex and gold, and 1 point per lot on indices, crude oil and crypto. Shares don’t earn points.To confirm

What is 130 points worth?

USD 5 cash back, paid into your trading account.

What are the smallest and biggest cash back amounts?

The smallest is USD 5 for 130 points. The biggest is USD 5,000 for 49,000 points.

Where can I see my points?

Your points balance is at the top of the client portal.

Where does my cash back go?

Into the trading account you choose when you redeem.

How long does it take?

Your cash back is processed within 1 to 2 working days.
