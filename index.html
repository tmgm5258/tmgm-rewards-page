Frequently asked questions

All 39 questions about TMGM Rewards in one place: earning, your points, cash back, gifts, delivery and the rules.

About TMGM Rewards

4 questions
Q1What is TMGM Rewards?

TMGM Rewards is our thank-you for the trading you already do. Your trades earn points, and you can swap them for cash back paid into your trading account, or for gifts. You’ll find it in your client portal, under TMGM Rewards in the sidebar.

Q2Who can join?

Clients aged 18 and over with a valid live trading account, in countries where TMGM Rewards is offered. It’s open to all types of live trading accounts, and eligibility is at TMGM’s discretion.

Q3What am I agreeing to when I take part?

By taking part, you agree to the TMGM Rewards Terms and Conditions and TMGM’s Client Agreement. TMGM uses your information in line with its Privacy Policy.

Q4Is TMGM Rewards available in my country?

TMGM Rewards is available in selected countries. To check whether it’s available where you live, contact client support at support@tmgm.com or your Account Manager.

Earning points

7 questions
Q5How do I earn points?

You earn points on the trades you make:

  • Forex and precious metals like gold: 10 points for every standard lot.
  • Indices, crude oil and crypto: 1 point per lot (each lot counts as 0.1 of a standard lot).
  • Shares, deposits and any other products don’t earn points.
Q6Which trades count?

Only closed trades held for 2 minutes or longer. High-frequency trading, meaning opening and closing trades at high frequency solely to generate commission, doesn’t earn points.

Q7Are points counted per trading account?To confirm

No. Points are counted across your whole client portal, not per trading account, and every account type counts the same way. Points are based on standard lots, so smaller lot sizes, such as cent accounts, earn proportionally less.

To confirmHow do cent accounts earn? BO is checking.

Q8When does earning start?To confirm

You start earning once TMGM Rewards is switched on for you. It’s switched on automatically, or when you ask us to. To ask, contact client support at support@tmgm.com or your Account Manager.

To confirmIs earning switched on automatically for every client?

Q9When will my points arrive?

Points usually arrive in your points wallet the next day.

Q10Can any accounts be left out of earning?

Yes. TMGM decides which trading accounts earn points and may leave some out, for example accounts taking part in other TMGM promotions.

Q11Why am I not earning points, or why don’t my points look right?

Contact client support at support@tmgm.com or your Account Manager. We’ll check your points and help sort it out.

Your points

6 questions
Q12Where can I see my points?

Your points balance is at the top of the client portal. You can also see it on the TMGM Rewards page.

Q13Do my points expire?To confirm

Yes. Points you haven’t redeemed expire after two years, whether or not you keep trading. Until then, you can redeem them while your account is active.

To confirmWhen do the two years start? The T&C doesn’t say.

Q14Can I transfer or sell my points?

No. Only the account holder can redeem points, and points can’t be transferred or sold.

Q15What happens to my points if the account holder passes away?

A family member, or someone who lives at the same address, can contact client support at support@tmgm.com to claim the remaining points within 12 months.

Q16Can I use points to pay trading fees or spreads?

No. Points can only be used in TMGM Rewards, not for trading fees, spreads or other trading costs.

Q17What happens to my points if my account is locked or deactivated?To confirm

While your account is locked, suspended or deactivated, you can’t use TMGM Rewards or redeem your points. Contact client support at support@tmgm.com or your Account Manager to check whether access can be restored.

If your access stays suspended for 12 months, for example because all your trading accounts are deactivated or you’ve left TMGM, your points expire.

To confirmDoes a locked account count as suspended, so the 12-month expiry applies?

Redeeming cash back

5 questions
Q18What is cash back?

Cash back lets you swap your points for US dollars, paid into a trading account you choose. Each cash back amount shows how many points it needs in TMGM Rewards.

Q19How many points do I need?

Check the points shown on each amount in TMGM Rewards, under Cash Back. The points needed can differ by region, so always go by the price shown in your own portal.

Q20How do I redeem cash back?
  1. Log in to the client portal and open TMGM Rewards in the sidebar.
  2. Go to Cash Back.
  3. Pick the amount you want.
  4. Press Redeem Now.
  5. Choose the trading account you want it paid into.
  6. Confirm.
Q21When will I get my cash back?

It’s paid instantly* into the trading account you chose, as soon as you confirm.

*Processing times may vary.

Q22Can I withdraw my cash back?

Yes. Cash back is added to your trading account as normal balance, so you can trade with it or withdraw it using the usual withdrawal steps and conditions.

Redeeming gifts

9 questions
Q23How do I redeem a gift?
  1. Log in to the client portal and open TMGM Rewards.
  2. Pick the item you want.
  3. Add your delivery address.
  4. Submit your order.

Your points are deducted as soon as you submit, so please check your order carefully.

Q24Is there a limit on how many items I can redeem?

There’s currently no limit per order, as long as you have enough points. Some limited-edition items have a quantity limit, shown on the item’s page.

Q25What are coupons?

Some promotions give you a coupon to redeem a specific item in TMGM Rewards. Only the account holder can use it. Each coupon shows its expiry date, can only be used once, and can’t be combined with other coupons.

Q26Can I choose the style, model or colour?

No. Items are bought as they’re listed, so we can’t take requests for a particular style, model or colour.

Q27Are the pictures exactly what I’ll receive?

Pictures are for reference only.

Q28What if an item runs out of stock?

Items depend on available stock, and we can’t guarantee an item will still be there when you order. If we can’t deliver your item, for example because it’s out of stock or there’s a delivery problem, the order can be cancelled and your points returned.

Q29Can I cancel or change my order?

Once you submit an order, it generally can’t be cancelled, changed or swapped for another item, because your points are deducted straight away. If we can’t deliver your item, the order can be cancelled and your points returned. If something has gone wrong with your order, contact client support at support@tmgm.com or your Account Manager.

Q30Can I change my delivery address after ordering?To confirm

You can change it once, before we start buying the item. Contact client support at support@tmgm.com or your Account Manager to check whether that’s still possible.

To confirmIs the one-time address change still allowed?

Q31Can I return or exchange a gift?To confirm

Please check the item details before you order. We can only take a return or exchange for a serious quality issue, not for minor defects, ordering mistakes or a change of mind. If your item has a serious quality issue, contact client support at support@tmgm.com as soon as it arrives.

To confirmIs there a time limit for reporting a serious quality issue?

Delivery

3 questions
Q32Where can you deliver my gift?

Only within the country registered in your client portal. We can’t ship to an address in another country.

Q33When will my gift ship?

We usually buy items once or twice a week, and shipping is usually arranged within 7 working days of your order. How long delivery takes after that depends on your local courier.

Q34What if my gift is late?

If your gift hasn’t arrived within the expected time, contact client support at support@tmgm.com or your Account Manager, and we’ll check the delivery for you.

Rules and security

5 questions
Q35Can I lose my points for misusing TMGM Rewards?

Yes. If we reasonably believe you’re misusing TMGM Rewards, for example through high-frequency trading solely to generate commission, fraud, or a breach of the Client Agreement or the TMGM Rewards Terms and Conditions, we can suspend or end your participation straight away and deduct your points.

Q36Can TMGM change the rewards or the rules?

Yes. We can change or remove items at any time and may not always let you know, so please check TMGM Rewards for what’s available. We can also suspend or end TMGM Rewards, and change its terms and conditions at any time. Updated terms are published online.

Q37How do you keep my points safe?

We take steps to protect your personal information and points, and points can’t be transferred. Please keep your portal login private. As set out in the Terms and Conditions, TMGM isn’t responsible for losses from redemptions made by someone else without your permission.

Q38Where are the full rules?

In the TMGM Rewards Terms and Conditions. If this FAQ and the Terms and Conditions ever differ, the Terms and Conditions apply.

Q39Who can I contact?

Client support at support@tmgm.com, or your Account Manager.
