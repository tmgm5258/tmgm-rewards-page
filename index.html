Help guide

Turn your points into cash back

TMGM Rewards lets you redeem your points for cash back.

130 points = USD 5, paid into your trading account.

This guide shows what your points are worth, where to check them and how to redeem in six simple steps.

01

What your points are worth

There are 12 cash back amounts, from USD 5 to USD 5,000.

  • 130points= USD 5
  • 240points= USD 10
  • 450points= USD 20
  • 600points= USD 30
  • 1,000points= USD 50
  • 1,850points= USD 100
  • 2,450points= USD 150
  • 4,300points= USD 250
  • 6,300points= USD 500
  • 11,500points= USD 1,000
  • 25,500points= USD 2,500
  • 49,000points= USD 5,000

Prices as shown in TMGM Rewards > Cash Back. Please check the store for the latest prices.

02

Where to check your points

Your points balance is at the top of the client portal. To see the cash back amounts, open TMGM Rewards in the sidebar and go to Cash Back.

  1. 1Your points balance
  2. 2TMGM Rewards, then Cash Back
03

How to redeem

  1. 1

    Log in to the client portal and open TMGM Rewards in the sidebar.

  2. 2

    Go to Cash Back.

  3. 3

    Pick the amount you want.

  4. 4

    Press Redeem Now.

  5. 5

    Choose the trading account you want it paid into.

  6. 6

    Confirm.

Your cash back is processed within 1 to 2 working days and paid into the trading account you chose.

04

Questions

What is 130 points worth?

USD 5 cash back, paid into your trading account.

What are the smallest and biggest cash back amounts?

The smallest is USD 5 for 130 points. The biggest is USD 5,000 for 49,000 points.

Where can I see my points?

Your points balance is at the top of the client portal.

Where does my cash back go?

Into the trading account you choose when you redeem.

How long does it take?

Your cash back is processed within 1 to 2 working days.

Why do some items show 999,999 points?

Those are promotion prizes. They need a coupon from a TMGM promotion, so they work differently from the 12 cash back amounts in this guide.
